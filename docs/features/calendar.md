## Calendar

The Events heading has Overview, BEOs and Production tabs. The old `/day-of` address redirects to Events › Overview.

`/calendar` (FLOW-63, ADR-19, FLOW-265; `src/features/calendar.tsx`, API `worker/routes/calendar.ts`) shows events at Negotiation or later (Negotiation, Closing, Closed, Preparation, Finished; never Lost or earlier stages) with FullCalendar's free month grid (`@fullcalendar/core`, `react`, `daygrid`, all MIT; `timegrid`, `list` stay installed). `tests/calendar.test.ts` fails if any other `@fullcalendar/*` package (the premium ones) is added.

- **Data:** `GET /api/calendar?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` (New York dates, inclusive, at most 62 days) returns the non-archived events at Negotiation or later overlapping the range that the caller may open (`visibleEvents`: salespeople their own, staff the events they work, FT+ roles all), with status, client, venue, guests, owner, lead, BEO and newest proposal (proposals only for `proposals.read`), the caller's own open tasks (assigned, overseeing or created; not done) due in the range, and the layers.
- **Times:** the API sends New York wall-clock times (`2026-09-17T17:00`); the calendar runs in UTC so it draws them unchanged in any browser time zone, and "now" is New York's wall clock.
- **Month view:** each event is a chip in its status color (FLOW-251) with its time and name. Filters: Owner, Status. Layers (Production tasks, Tentative holds, Staff shifts, and My Google Calendar once connected) are one "Show: N layers" dropdown (FLOW-332) whose options have rounded-square checkboxes (`checkboxes` and `summary` on the shared `Dropdown`); several can be on, and all calendar layers are off until checked. Clicking an event opens a side panel (status, date and time, client, venue, guests, owner, proposal) with Open event, Open Proposal (`proposals.read`), Open BEO and Open lead (`leads.read`). Clicking a task opens the task.
- **List view:** the month's events as the events table (Status, Date & Time, Event, Client, Venue, Guests, Owner) with inline edits (FLOW-261) and selection (FLOW-260; events with a signed contract or an approved BEO are skipped). Bulk: Change owner, Change status. No "New event": events start from a lead.
- The view, month, filters and layers are kept in the address (`?view=list&date=&owner=&status=&layers=`).

**Google Calendar** (FLOW-268; `shared/google-calendar.ts`, `worker/google/`, `worker/records/google-calendar.ts`, routes `worker/routes/google-calendar.ts`): each person may connect their own Google account from the calendar's filter bar ("Connect Google Calendar", shown once the Google secrets are set).

- **Connect:** `POST /api/google-calendar/connect` answers Google's consent URL (scopes `calendar.events.readonly` and `calendar.app.created`, offline access); Google sends the browser to `GET /api/google-calendar/callback`, which is public and trusts only the signed `state` (HMAC with `GOOGLE_TOKEN_KEY`, 10 minutes). The refresh token is stored AES-GCM encrypted under `GOOGLE_TOKEN_KEY` and never returned to the browser. Connecting creates a "Flowbase events" calendar in the person's account.
- **My Google Calendar:** `GET /api/google-calendar/events?from=&to=` reads the person's primary calendar for the range and answers only them; nothing is stored, and answers are cached in memory 5 minutes per person and range. On the calendar they draw as the "My Google Calendar" layer, on by default once connected (`?gcal=0` keeps it off), outlined in blue, and open in Google.
- **Push:** a `google_calendar.sync` job copies every event the person may open on the Flowbase calendar (Negotiation and later, the last 30 days and the next year) to "Flowbase events", updates changed ones (a fingerprint per copy in `google_calendar_event_links`) and removes those that went Lost, were deleted or left their view. Event creates, updates and status changes queue a sync for everyone connected, and the cron queues one per person every 15 minutes. Each job makes at most 8 Google writes and queues a follow-up for the rest.
- **Disconnect:** `DELETE /api/google-calendar` revokes the token at Google, deletes it here and stops pushing; the "Flowbase events" calendar stays in Google. When Google refuses the refresh token (revoked, or the 7-day limit of an app in Testing), the API answers 409 `GOOGLE_RECONNECT` and the calendar offers "Reconnect Google Calendar".
- **Setup:** an OAuth client of type Web application with redirect URI `${APP_URL}/api/google-calendar/callback`, then `npx wrangler secret put GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `GOOGLE_TOKEN_KEY` (`openssl rand -base64 32`) `--env production`. Without all three and `APP_URL`, the controls stay hidden.

**BEOs** (`/beos`, FLOW-265; `shared/beo-list.ts`, `worker/records/beo-list.ts`, `src/features/beo-list.tsx`): `GET /api/beos?when=upcoming|past|all&status=draft|awaiting_approval|approved|final&owner=<userId>` lists the BEO of every event the caller may open, with version, status, guests, owner, last editor, last update and the newest approved PDF saved in Documents. Final is derived: an approved BEO whose event is Finished (FLOW-265 A1). Open shows a side preview with Open BEO, Print and Open in Documents (`/documents?document=<id>` opens that file's preview). Read only: BEO content is edited on the BEO page.

**Production** (`/production`, FLOW-265; `worker/records/production.ts`, `src/features/production.tsx`): `GET /api/production/overview?days=7|14|30` returns the work of the next 7, 14 or 30 days grouped by day (tasks, pulls, kitchen prep, shortages, staffing, trucks, orders, setup; each item with its event's date) and the open readiness questions of those events, which the page answers or dismisses in its panel. Tasks are checked off in place; shortages offer "Add to order list" (a draft order list from the order-by day through the event). Filters: Days, Team, Type, Event.
