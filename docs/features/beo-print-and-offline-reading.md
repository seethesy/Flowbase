## BEO print and offline reading (FLOW-149, FLOW-150)

- **Print (FLOW-149):** the BEO's Print button opens `/#/print/events/<event id>/beo` (`src/features/beo-print.tsx`), a shell-less page on the shared `PrintLayout` that lists the ten sections in `beoSections` order from the viewer's own role projection, so a part-time user's printout has no client or venue phone or email. Layout images print at full width (the annotated PNG when there is one). The footer, repeated on every page through the layout's `footer` prop, shows the BEO status and the time the page was opened. Users print or "Save as PDF" from the browser; compare a printout with the team's current BEO by hand.
- **Offline (FLOW-150, ADR-18):** `vite-plugin-pwa` (Workbox) precaches the app so it opens with no signal, in a normal Safari tab; API calls are never cached by the service worker. Each time Flowbase opens online, `src/offline/sync.ts` reads `GET /api/offline-beos` (the approved BEOs, projected for the person's role, of events they lead, assist or are rostered on that start today through the next `OFFLINE_BEO_DAYS` = 7 days) and saves them in IndexedDB (Dexie, `flowbase-offline`) with their layout and setup photos as blobs. With no signal the app signs in from the saved profile and shows `/#/offline`: a list of saved BEOs that open read-only, with "Available offline · last synced …". Signing out (or another person signing in on the device) removes the saved BEOs, photos and profile. Photos are fetched by the browser from the signed R2 URLs, so the bucket's CORS rules must allow `GET` from the app's origin; a photo that can't be fetched is skipped. Safari may clear site data after about a week unused, so readiness checks (F-16-02) remind staff to open Flowbase the day before. The offline Playwright test runs in WebKit (`webkit-offline` project); other projects block the service worker.
- **New deployments (FLOW-325):** the service worker runs in `prompt` mode, so a new version installs and is precached in the background (offline reading keeps working) and then waits. `src/app/appUpdate.ts` (started from `src/main.tsx`) checks for a new version every 5 minutes and when the tab comes back into view (at most once a minute). A new version found within 10 seconds of opening takes over at once, so a new tab or fresh load shows it on the first or second load. Later it takes over on the next navigation, or when the person presses Reload on the "New version of Flowbase available" notice (`AppUpdateNotice`, above each page); it never reloads by itself while there are unsaved edits (`src/app/unsavedEdits.ts`: pending live edits, an open drawer or dialog, or a focused field). A lazy screen chunk that a deploy removed reloads the app once (`vite:preloadError`, and `RouteError` on the routes); a second failure within a minute shows "Unable to display this page" instead of looping. `public/_headers` makes `sw.js` and `index.html` always revalidate and caches `/assets/*` (hashed) for a year.
