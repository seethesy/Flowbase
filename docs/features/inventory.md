## Inventory

Rental details, decor kits, service offerings and supplies (FLOW-72/79/80/92): rental forms store internal/replacement costs, dimensions, assembly notes, buffers and a color list. A single rental item can hold separate quantities per color and section; movements, counts, transfers and corrections preserve the color. Existing uncolored balances remain in an explicit unassigned-color bucket. A color with stock cannot be removed. Staff and Kitchen Staff never receive rental costs, including full-time users. Empty item ratios inherit the current category default from Settings; item overrides take precedence. Admin and Sales Manager can edit rental prices directly (FLOW-73's settled role exception); other roles cannot directly reprice rentals while approval proposals remain pending implementation in FLOW-73.

Decor rentals can define kits of rental/consumable components, with active-reference and cycle validation. Services support fixed, per-person and quoted prices, per-person add-ons and fixed/per-guest includes; they show no stock quantities, including in CSV exports. Photos continue to use the existing item attachments. Supplies are Master items whose stock is used up at events (FLOW-289); they're listed on the Inventory tab with their par, pack size, Last counted and Last counted by. `shared/inventory-requirements.ts` provides pure ratio fallback and nested kit/service quantity calculations.

Event integration (FLOW-212/213/214). Holding a kit holds each of its rental parts in a part hold of its own (`reservations.kit_reservation_id`), at the kit's quantity times what one kit takes, nested kits opened up; the parts follow the kit's hold and can't be edited on their own. Its consumable parts become event requirements with source `kit`. A kit has no stock: its hold and its availability (`/inventory/items/:id/availability`, which lists the parts) come from its scarcest part, a short part opens a conflict that names the part and the kit (only "accept the risk" applies to it; change the kit's hold otherwise), and the kit's page lists its parts' conflicts. On the pull list and the BEO a kit's parts sit in the kit's group (Decor for a decor kit), so closeout records the candles' use and the glassware's return once each and nothing for the kit. Sending a proposal also holds its decor lines that are catalog rentals and the rentals its services include (fixed and per-guest); the pull list already lists a service's rentals and consumables. A service's task template is linked from the template (`matchCatalogId`, FLOW-138), shown on the service's page, and applied once when planning starts. Supplies flow through FLOW-135 requirements, FLOW-87 order suggestions (par gap) and the FLOW-78 import (Sternos as supplies). Not built: holding a kit part in a specific color.

Inventory items (FLOW-69), locations and stock (FLOW-70, FLOW-289), the ingredient catalog (FLOW-81), the stock ledger (FLOW-71), count mode (FLOW-82), cost history (FLOW-83) and bar stock (FLOW-90) live in Postgres and are served by `worker/routes/inventory.ts`; Zod schemas in `shared/inventory.ts` are shared by the API and the screens. The Inventory heading's tabs are Inventory (`/inventory`), Bar Inventory (`/inventory/bar`), Order (`/inventory/order`, `src/features/inventory-order.tsx`), Vendors and Photos; Kitchen › Ingredients is `/kitchen/ingredients`; an item opens at `/inventory/<id>`; count mode is `/inventory/counts/<id>` (`src/features/stock-counts.tsx`), started from Start stock count on the Inventory and Bar Inventory tabs; Settings › Locations is `/settings/locations` and Settings › Imports `/settings/imports`. The old URLs redirect: `/purchasing`, `/inventory/orders` and `/inventory/low-stock` to the Order tab, `/kitchen/orders` to its kitchen table, `/inventory/ingredients` to `/kitchen/ingredients`, `/inventory/master`, `/inventory/supplies` and `/inventory/counts` to `/inventory`, `/inventory/sections` to Settings › Locations and `/imports` to Settings › Imports (FLOW-291). Item lists are paged (see [Core records](core-records.md#core-records)) and every item filter runs in SQL (FLOW-180).

- **Three lists (FLOW-289):** items live in `master_inventory_items` (rentals, decor, supplies and services), `bar_inventory_items` (bar stock) and `kitchen_ingredients`, which share their columns; a CHECK keeps each kind in its own table. `all_inventory_items` is a read-only `UNION ALL` view (`security_invoker`) for reads that span lists; writes and row locks go to the item's own table (`itemTableOf`, `lockItemRow` in `worker/records/inventory-common.ts`). References that can name an item from any list (stock, movements, count lines, cost history, requirements, allocations, consumable details) have no foreign key; the rest point at their own table. An item's `list` is `master`, `bar` or `kitchen`. Every Master item reads "Rental"; its internal stock tracking (`stockTrackingOf`) maps the kind: rentals are returned after events, consumable supplies are used up at events (they still consume and reorder) and services are not stocked. Only Admin picks it, on a new item. Migration `0076` emptied the old table and every row that depended on it (recipes keep their dishes without ingredient lines).

- **Items:** `kind` is `rental`, `consumable` or `service`, with a subtype for rentals (equipment, furniture, linen, tableware, glassware, bar, decor) and consumables (ingredient, bar, supply). Every item has a code, name, category, stock unit, optional display group, tier and color/finish, and an optional ratio (e.g. 2 per guest). Kind-specific fields live in their own tables (`inventory_rental_details`, `inventory_consumable_details`, `inventory_service_details`). An item's kind can't change, and a live code is unique. Its stock unit can't change once it has any stock movement, even at a zero balance (`409 UNIT_LOCKED`, FLOW-198): movements keep no unit of their own, so the change would reinterpret them (10 lb as 10 oz). The check runs under the item lock that `recordMovement` also takes, so a first movement and a unit change can't interleave; there is no automatic conversion. Photos attach through [Attachments](attachments.md#attachments) (`inventory_item`). Categories use business settings (FLOW-45); allergens are set on dishes, not ingredients (FLOW-95). A consumable's preferred vendor is picked from the [vendor directory](vendors.md#vendors) (`preferredVendorId`, with its name copied to `preferredVendor`), or typed as free text for a vendor that isn't in it.
- **Item form (FLOW-317):** `ItemForm` (`src/features/inventory/inventory-item-form.tsx`) keeps the state and save; each part is a section component beside it: classification (`inventory-item-classification.tsx`), rental (`inventory-item-rental.tsx`), service (`inventory-item-service.tsx`) and consumable/bar (`inventory-item-consumable.tsx`). `buildItemInput` (`inventory-item-input.ts`) is the pure builder of the API input from the form state, unit-tested per kind in `tests/inventory/inventory-item-input.test.ts`.
- **Units** (`shared/units.ts`, FLOW-51): count (each, dozen, case), weight (oz, lb, g, kg) and volume (tsp, tbsp, cup, fl oz, qt, gal, ml, l). `convert()` handles the standard conversions plus each item's pack count (a case of 12) and "one each is about" size (750 ml a bottle, 8 oz a chicken breast), and returns `cannot_convert` instead of guessing (weight ↔ volume never converts). `orderQuantity()` rounds up to whole purchase units. A recipe unit an ingredient can't be converted to is flagged on the form (`recipeUnitProblem`).
- **Stock:** quantities are per item and location (FLOW-289). Master and Bar items are kept at locations (Warehouse, Storage Box, Jamie's - Garage, Jamie's - Basement, Jamie's - Main Floor, Cozy Cafe, and Vendor for sub-rentals), which Admin edits in Settings › Locations; kitchen ingredients keep their own storage list (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry). The defaults are seeded by migration.
- **Stock ledger (FLOW-71):** every quantity change is a `stock_movements` row: one item, one section, a signed quantity, a type (`receive`, `adjust`, `count`, `transfer`, `pull`, `return`, `damage_loss`, `consume`), the reason, the user and time, and an optional link to an event, order or import. A transfer is two rows sharing a `transfer_id`. `inventory_stock` caches on-hand per item and section; `recordMovement` (`worker/records/inventory.ts`) is the only writer and updates it in the same transaction as the movements, never lets a section go below zero, and rejects service items. No route sets a quantity. Movements are append-only (a database trigger rejects updates and deletes); a correction is a new movement of the difference that points at the original (`corrects_id`), and a transfer is corrected by transferring back. Each movement emits `stock.moved` and writes one `stock_movement` audit row in its transaction (FLOW-168). Importers enter quantities with `recordImportedStock`: a `count` movement for the difference between the file's quantity and the section's on-hand, linked to the import's log (`link_type` `import`). Re-running an import sets the section back to the file's quantity, even if stock moved since; a matching quantity records nothing.
- **Count mode (FLOW-82, FLOW-291):** a count covers one location (or kitchen storage area): its stocked items, rentals and bar stock included (kept there, holding stock there, or already counted), listed in shelf order (`storageOrder` on the item). Each saved count writes a `count` movement for the difference (bar counts round to the nearest 0.5 for beer, wine and champagne and the nearest 0.25 for liquor and cordials, `barCountStep`) and replaces the item's line in the count (`stock_counts`, `stock_count_lines`), so a count left part-way resumes where it stopped; starting a count where one is open resumes it. Consumable counts need no approval; a rental is counted here only by someone who can change Master inventory directly (`rentals.apply_directly`), and otherwise from the item page through approval.
- **Order tab (FLOW-292):** `/inventory/order` shows Order lists and Approvals at the top, the order lists table, and one reorder table per list (Inventory, Bar Inventory, and Kitchen ingredients at `#kitchen`) with the items at or below par (on hand at or under a par above zero; services never appear): On hand, Par, Needed, Bought as, Unit cost, Vendor (Distributor for bar), Est. cost. Add to order list, per row or for the selected rows, puts the items on the newest draft order list with what they need (one purchase unit for an item exactly at par), replacing a line they already have, or starts a new draft for today.
- **History (FLOW-185):** creating, editing, archiving and restoring an item, and creating, renaming, archiving and restoring a section, write an audit row in the same transaction (`inventory_item`, `inventory_section`). Item snapshots are the item's API shape without stock (stock is the ledger's), so a price change records only `rental`, `service` or `consumable`. `GET /api/inventory/items/<id>/history` hides consumable costs from users who can't see costs; a cost-only change doesn't appear for them.
- **Cost history (FLOW-83):** every change to a consumable's unit cost is logged in `inventory_cost_history` with the previous and new cost, the source (`manual` from the item form; `receiving` from FLOW-88 through `recordCostChange`) and the user, and emits `inventory_item.cost_changed` for recipe costing (FLOW-94) to recalculate recipes.
- **Bar stock (FLOW-90):** consumables of subtype `bar`, with a category from the bar inventory masterlist (Beer, Wine, Champagne, Liquor, Cordials, Mixers & Juices, Garnishes, Soft Beverages), a subcategory for wine (White, Red) and liquor (Vodka, Tequila, Gin, Rum, Whiskey, Cognac), with category lists managed by business settings (FLOW-45), brand or varietal and container (`inventory_bar_details`), tier `rail`, `mid`, `high` or `premium`, size (each size), pack size (case count), distributor (preferred vendor), unit cost and par. Bar stock is still kept per location, but no bar screen shows the location (FLOW-291). The bar screen filters by category, subcategory, tier and distributor.
- **Who can do what** (`worker/policy.ts`): every signed-in user can read inventory. Admin, Technical, Sales Manager and the kitchen lead can change items and consumable stock and correct movements. Rental stock (counts, receipts, transfers, corrections) is also updated by salespeople, kitchen staff and FT+ (`canMoveItem`, `canCountItem`); editing the rental item itself stays with the managers. Ingredients are counted by Admin, the kitchen lead and kitchen staff; bar stock and supplies by Admin and FT+. Only Admin adds, renames, reorders and archives locations; a location that still holds stock can't be archived. Kitchen Ingredients follow the kitchen's permissions (FLOW-289): `recipes.read` to list them and `recipes.write` to change them. Consumable costs and cost history are visible to FT+, Kitchen Lead and Kitchen Staff (FLOW-39): costs come back `null` (with `costHidden`) for anyone else, and an update that omits the cost keeps it.
