Lead Market

The Lead Market features event requests which were not sent to you directly. These Eventective users are planning an event in your area and have requested to hear from businesses like yours. Find new Prospects in your area by exploring our competitive Lead Market.

Leads are displayed in the order received, with the most recent Leads at the top. Browse the list for event details such as attendee count, event date, location, and budget (if provided). Use Lead Filters to further qualify your Leads.

Select any Lead to view more details. If you'd like to pursue the Lead, select "To Inbox" to purchase the Lead and move it to your Inbox. Not interested? Select "Delete" to move the Lead to your Deleted folder and clear it from the Lead Market.

Any purchased Leads will be available in your Inbox and marked as a Prospect|Lead.
